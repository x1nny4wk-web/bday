💭 A Letter Written for You

If I could gather every moment of gratitude I've ever felt and place it into words, this letter would still not be enough. Thank you for being a friend whose presence makes every path a little easier to walk. Thank you for taking me beyond the places I already knew, showing me roads I had never walked and moments I might never have experienced on my own. When I look back someday, I know I will remember them fondly, not because of where we went, but because of who was beside me.

I'm also sorry for all the unnecessary drama between us, even over the smallest things. For the misunderstandings, the arguments, and all the moments when something simple somehow became more complicated than it needed to be. We may not always understand each other, and we may annoy each other more than we'd like to admit, but despite all of that, I'm grateful that we're still here. Somehow, through all the chaos, our friendship always finds its way back.

As time carries us toward different futures, I hope your dreams never lose their wings. I hope you find the places you've always wanted to see, achieve the things you've worked so hard for, and find the happiness you so easily give to others. You deserve beautiful things, and wherever life takes you, I hope you never forget that there was once a friend who was deeply grateful to have walked a small part of the journey beside you.

- Indi -
